## About Me

I’m an Environmental and Agricultural Development graduate focused on **crop valorization, agro-processing, and sustainable agriculture**. I’m particularly interested in reducing post-harvest losses, extending shelf life, and developing value-added products to strengthen food security and sustainable food systems.

I’m drawn to the intersection of **agricultural research, food innovation, technology, and entrepreneurship**. My long-term professional direction combines crop valorization and agricultural innovation with agro-processing, product development, and post-harvest loss reduction. Grounded in agricultural research, evidence-based development, and entrepreneurship across the agricultural value chain, my career interests span research institutions, NGOs, international development organizations, and private agribusiness.
